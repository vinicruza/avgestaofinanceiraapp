# Asset inventory — no-capture path

The live site (avgestaofinanceira.com.br) blocks automated capture (HTTP 403 /
network policy blocks the tunnel), so no screenshots or DOM assets were pulled.
Brand identity was reconstructed faithfully from the app's own source code in
this repository (colors, fonts, and verbatim copy).

## Available assets
None as image files. Client logos referenced by the site (Ipiranga, Petrobras,
BR Mania, ampm, Pit Stop) are hosted externally by Lovable and are not present
as local files, and are unreachable through the network policy. They will be
represented as styled text wordmarks rather than logo images (also cleaner and
avoids trademark-logo rendering).

## Brand signals (from source)
- Palette: navy-deep `#0b1120`, navy `#141d33`, vibrant brand blue `#3a6df5`,
  strong blue `#2a56dd`, white `#ffffff`, off-white `#eef2fb`.
- Type: Inter (display weights 800/900 extrabold; body 400–600).
- Voice: confident, modern, B2B; "operações financeiras inteligentes".
- Visual feel: dark navy hero, radial blue glows, geometric, typographic,
  no stock photography.
